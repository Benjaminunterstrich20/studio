# **App Name**: flash

## Core Features:

- Prioritized News Feed: Displays important announcements and news at the top of the homepage.
- Category-Based Navigation: Organizes content into clear categories such as 'News', 'Announcements', 'School Life', and 'Events'.
- Article Management: Enables administrators to create, edit, and categorize articles with titles, content, optional images, and author attribution.
- Event Calendar: Displays upcoming school events with dates, times, and descriptions.
- Permanent link to the school: Link to the official school website (opens in a new tab)
- Search and Archive: Allows users to search for specific articles and access an archive of older content.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft red (#F44336) to highlight important information and CTAs without being overwhelming.
- Background color: White (#FFFFFF) to maintain a clean, spacious layout.
- Secondary color: Calming blue (#64B5F6) to promote trust and clarity, echoing a modern educational environment.
- Body text: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a modern, clear, and readable style.
- Headline text: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for headlines and short amounts of body text; if longer text is anticipated, use 'Inter' for body
- Card-based design for articles to provide a structured and clean visual hierarchy.
- Extensive use of whitespace to enhance readability and create a calm, modern feel.
- Simple, clear icons for categories and navigation to improve usability.
